The Insight
RADAR
MEETS
MARKETS
In 1979, Donald Reid published a paper on Multiple Hypothesis Tracking — a method for tracking objects through radar clutter without prematurely collapsing uncertainty. The insight: don't commit to one interpretation until the evidence is overwhelming. Maintain a tree of possibilities. Prune the unlikely ones. Let the data decide.

The Adaptation

Replace radar targets with trading strategies. Replace clutter with market noise. Replace O(n²) hypothesis merging with FAISS sub-linear ANN search. The math transfers. The architecture is new.
