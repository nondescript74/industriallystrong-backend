GUT

A multi-agent AI system that analyzes foods for FODMAP content, IBS triggers, and dietary risk — using parallel Claude + Gemini analysis with synthesis reconciliation.
IBS
The Problem
FOOD IS
COMPLEX
IBS affects 10–15% of the global population. FODMAP sensitivity varies by serving size, preparation method, and individual triggers. Static food databases can't capture this — you need reasoning, not just lookup.
🌾
FructansGarlic, onion, wheat — threshold-dependent triggers hiding in everyday foods
🥛
LactoseNot just milk — soft cheese, yogurt, cream sauces all vary by strain and process
🍑
PolyolsStone fruits, artificial sweeteners — dose-dependent and cumulative through the day
🫘
GOS & FructoseLegumes, honey, HFCS — context-dependent absorption changes everything
AI
The Architecture
THREE
AGENTS
ONE ANSWER
Two frontier AI models analyze every query independently and in parallel. A synthesis agent reconciles disagreements into a unified assessment with confidence tiers.
🧠
Claude
Anthropic's frontier model. Deep reasoning about ingredient interactions and citation-backed risk.
🔬
Gemini
Google's multimodal model. Independent FODMAP analysis with vision support for food photos.
⚖️
Synthesis
Reconciliation engine. Resolves disagreements and produces unified assessment.
