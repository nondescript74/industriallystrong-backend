Going Global
How a Director of Program Management transformed a resource-starved engineering organization by building international partnerships from the ground up.
Zahirudeen Premji — Director of Program Management, LSI Storage
The Situation
Never Enough Hands
LSI Logic's storage division was perennially under-resourced. Projects piled up faster than teams could deliver. Technical debt climbed. Engineering management guarded the firmware codebase tightly — but the software layer was wide open and critically understaffed.

The Strategic Shift
See the Opening. Take It.
Engineering wouldn't release the firmware. So I reframed the problem: the products had a significant software component that was equally starved for resources. I proposed going to India — not to outsource, but to find genuine engineering partners for the software work that was falling behind.
Reporting directly to the EVP of LSI Storage, I had the executive air cover to make the case.
Leveraging Networks
The Right Connection
I brought in a strategic consultant — an executive who had built US West's cellular business connections across India. Through her deep network of C-suite relationships, we secured executive-level meetings at five of India's most prominent IT services firms.
