OurFolly

Every director gets a cockpit that remembers.

Boards turn over, managers change and email piles up. OurFolly keeps the association's records, decisions and history in one place, filed by the five questions every director asks.

WhoWhatWhenWhereWhy

Ask, and it reconciles

When the manager sends a reply with a table or attaches financials, one question checks it against the records and shows what does not match.

Nothing lost in a handover

If the management company changes, everything in its portal that belongs to the association comes into OurFolly first.

Each director's own office

President, treasurer and the rest each open their own view, with the tasks and history that role needs.

In use by board directors today. For HOA and condo boards and any group run by volunteers. You can try a live cockpit for Aspen Hollow, a made-up HOA, with a personal evaluation link.
