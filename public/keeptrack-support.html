Frequently Asked Questions

What can I track with KeepTrack?

KeepTrack is designed to be flexible. You can track habits, goals, expenses, health metrics, workouts, mood, or anything else that's important to you.

How do I create a new tracker?

Tap the '+' button and choose what you want to track. You can customize the name, icon, unit of measurement, and tracking frequency.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Export your tracking data as CSV files for use in spreadsheets or other analysis tools.

Is my tracking data private?

Completely. Your data is stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud account. We don't collect or access your personal tracking information.

System Requirements
