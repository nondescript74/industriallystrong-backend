Features

  • OCR Technology: Scan recipe cards and cookbook pages using advanced vision recognition
  • FODMAP Analysis: Identify high-FODMAP ingredients for dietary management
  • Allergen Detection: Track and flag common allergens in your recipes
  • Smart Search: Find recipes by ingredient, dietary needs, or category
  • Privacy First: Your data stays on your devices with optional iCloud sync

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my recipes across devices?

Reczipes uses CloudKit to automatically sync your recipes across all your Apple devices when you're signed in with the same Apple ID. Make sure iCloud is enabled in Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud.

How does OCR scanning work?

Use your camera to capture recipe cards or cookbook pages. Our advanced Vision framework extracts text automatically. You can then edit and save the recipe to your collection.

What is FODMAP analysis?

FODMAP analysis helps identify ingredients that may trigger digestive issues. The app flags high-FODMAP ingredients in your recipes, making it easier to manage dietary restrictions.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your recipes are stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud account. We don't have access to your data, and we don't collect analytics or personal information.

How do I export or share recipes?

You can share individual recipes via email with full formatting. Recipients can easily import shared recipes into their own Reczipes app.
